📊 Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the Yuva Intern VA Virtual Internship Program to apply Data Analytics and Data Science techniques to a logistics and supply chain use case.

The project focuses on analyzing logistics data to understand operational performance, identify demand and inventory patterns, evaluate delivery efficiency, perform predictive analysis, and generate data-driven insights for logistics decision-making.

The project was completed across four weeks, covering the complete analytics workflow from strategic planning and data preparation to exploratory analysis, forecasting, optimization, and final business insights.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- Analyze logistics and supply chain data.
- Clean, validate, and prepare data for analysis.
- Identify demand, inventory, order, and delivery patterns.
- Define and evaluate relevant logistics KPIs.
- Perform exploratory data analysis and visualization.
- Develop and evaluate demand forecasting approaches.
- Analyze inventory-related risks.
- Perform product or customer segmentation where applicable.
- Explore transportation and route optimization opportunities.
- Generate meaningful business insights.
- Support data-driven logistics planning and decision-making.

---

⚠️ Business Problem

Logistics organizations need to maintain appropriate inventory levels while meeting customer demand, fulfilling orders on time, and operating transportation efficiently.

Common operational challenges include:

- Inaccurate demand estimation
- Stock-outs and excess inventory
- Delayed deliveries
- Inefficient order fulfillment
- Poor resource utilization
- Inefficient transportation and routing
- Limited visibility into operational performance

Project Approach:
The project uses a structured, data-driven analytical approach to understand these challenges and support informed logistics decision-making.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

KPI| Purpose
On-Time Delivery Rate (OTD)| Measures the proportion of deliveries completed on or before the expected delivery time.
Inventory Turnover| Indicates how efficiently inventory is utilized and replenished.
Stock-Out Rate| Measures the occurrence of inventory shortages.
Mean Absolute Error (MAE)| Measures the average absolute difference between actual and forecasted demand.
Order Fill Rate| Measures the proportion of customer demand fulfilled from available inventory.
Average Delivery Distance| Measures the average transportation distance associated with deliveries.
