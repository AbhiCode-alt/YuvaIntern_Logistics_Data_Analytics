RESEARCH SOURCES USED

1. Kaggle — M5 Forecasting - Accuracy
https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/m5-forecasting-accuracy/data
Use: daily sales, store/item hierarchy, calendar and price data for demand forecasting.

2. UCI Machine Learning Repository — Online Retail II
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online%2Bretail
Use: transaction-level retail analysis, product/customer analysis and demand exploration.

3. Google Developers — OR-Tools
https://developers.google.com/optimization
Use: optimization framework for routing, flows, linear/integer programming and constraint programming.

4. Google Developers — Vehicle Routing
https://developers.google.com/optimization/routing
Use: vehicle routing concepts, capacity constraints and route optimization.

5. Google Developers — Vehicle Routing with Time Windows
https://developers.google.com/optimization/routing/vrptw
Use: delivery time-window modeling.

6. ASCM Insights — The 10 Essential KPIs for Supply Chain
https://www.ascm.org/ascm-insights/the-10-essential-kpis-for-supply-chain/
Use: supply-chain KPI context, including on-time supply/delivery concepts.
