YUVA INTERN VA – LOGISTICS DATA ANALYTICS

Week 1: Strategic Planning and Data Exploration


1. Project Overview

This project is developed as part of the Yuva Intern VA Virtual Internship Program. It focuses on applying data analytics and data science methods to a logistics and supply chain use case.

The primary objective is to analyze logistics data and develop a structured analytical framework for improving demand planning, inventory management, delivery performance, and resource utilization.


2. Business Problem

Logistics operations require accurate demand planning, appropriate inventory levels, timely order fulfillment, and efficient transportation. Poor demand estimation can lead to stock-outs or excess inventory, while inefficient delivery planning can increase transportation time and operational costs.

This project proposes a data-driven analytical framework to identify operational patterns, measure key performance indicators, and support informed logistics decision-making.


3. Project Objectives

- Analyze logistics and supply chain data to identify relevant patterns and trends.
- Define and evaluate key operational and business performance indicators.
- Analyze demand, inventory, order fulfillment, and delivery patterns.
- Establish a suitable approach for demand forecasting.
- Identify potential areas for inventory risk reduction.
- Analyze opportunities for transportation and route optimization.
- Apply Python-based data analytics and visualization techniques.
- Provide data-driven insights to support logistics decision-making.


4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- On-Time Delivery Rate (OTD)
- Inventory Turnover
- Stock-Out Rate
- Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
- Order Fill Rate
- Average Delivery Distance


5. Analytical Approach

The proposed analytical workflow consists of the following stages:

1. Business Understanding
2. Data Collection
3. Data Cleaning and Validation
4. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
5. Feature Engineering
6. Demand Forecasting
7. Inventory Risk Analysis
8. Product and Customer Segmentation
9. Route Optimization
10. KPI Evaluation
11. Decision Support

8. Expected Outcome

The proposed analytical framework is expected to provide a structured basis for:

- Understanding historical demand and operational patterns.
- Monitoring inventory-related risks.
- Evaluating delivery and order fulfillment performance.
- Identifying operational inefficiencies.
- Supporting better resource allocation.
- Identifying potential opportunities for route optimization.
- Supporting data-driven logistics planning and decision-making.


9. Scope and Limitation

Week 1 is focused on strategic planning, data exploration, and analytical design. The actual performance improvements and business impact will be assessed only after applying the proposed methodology to a suitable logistics dataset and evaluating the resulting analytical findings.
